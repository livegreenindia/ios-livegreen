Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap Forgot Password? and enter your email. You will receive a password reset link within a few minutes.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 7 days. You can also email us at apps@livegreenindia.com to request deletion.

How do I connect Apple Health (iOS)?

Go to Profile → Apple Health and tap Connect. LiveGreen will request permission to read your steps, calories, and activity data from Apple Health.

My activities are not tracking correctly

Make sure location permissions are set to Always for trek recording. For general activity tracking, ensure the app has motion permission enabled in your device Settings.

How do I report a bug?

Email us at apps@livegreenindia.com with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS/Android version. Screenshots or screen recordings are very helpful.

Sign In with Apple / Google not working

Ensure you have a stable internet connection. For Sign In with Apple, make sure you are signed in to your Apple ID on the device. If the issue persists, email us at apps@livegreenindia.com.
